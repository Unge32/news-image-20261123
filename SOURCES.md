## Pinned stories (numbered on the globe)

1. **COP31 COLLAPSES — Antalya, Turkey. No fossil fuel deal.**
   The COP31 climate conference in Antalya collapsed on Sunday
   22 November 2026: the EU, Panama, the Marshall Islands and Norway
   rejected the "Antalya Pact" because it contained no language on
   moving away from fossil fuels; COP31 president Murat Kurum
   acknowledged there was no consensus. Longest COP in history, first
   major breakdown since COP15 Copenhagen 2009.
   Sources: CNN, 22 Nov 2026 ("Global climate summit collapses as
   countries fail to agree on moving away from fossil fuels"); Wall
   Street Journal, 23 Nov 2026 ("U.N. Climate Talks Collapse Over
   Fossil Fuel Rift"); Le Monde, 22 Nov 2026.
